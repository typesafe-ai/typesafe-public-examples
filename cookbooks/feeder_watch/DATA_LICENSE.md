# Data licence

The frames in `usfws_frames.zip` are from the video [Busy Bird Feeder](https://www.flickr.com/photos/usfwsmidwest/31524812531) by Courtney Celley, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, published under the [Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/). Changes: one frame cut per second, scaled to 1024 × 576 pixels and saved as JPEG.

`usfws_labels.json` and the other files this cookbook publishes to typesafe-public-examples are TypeSafe's work under that repository's MIT licence.
