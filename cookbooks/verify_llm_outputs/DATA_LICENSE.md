# Data licence

`musique_questions.json` is drawn from the development set of [MuSiQue](https://github.com/StonyBrookNLP/musique) v1.0, *MuSiQue: Multihop Questions via Single-hop Question Composition*, by Harsh Trivedi, Niranjan Balasubramanian, Tushar Khot and Ashish Sabharwal, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Its paragraphs are Wikipedia text. Changes: a random sample of 1,000 questions from the full development set, keeping one of the two versions MuSiQue publishes for each, with each question's paragraphs, reference answer and answer aliases. The paragraphs' supporting-fact flags and the question decompositions were dropped.

The other files this cookbook publishes to typesafe-public-examples are TypeSafe's work under that repository's MIT licence.
