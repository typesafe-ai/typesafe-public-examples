# Data licence

The court-opinion passages and queries in `json_cache.json` (the `build_slice` entry) are U.S. federal court opinions from the [Caselaw Access Project](https://case.law/) (Harvard Law School Library), released under [CC0 1.0](https://case.law/terms/), as sampled from [CLERC](https://huggingface.co/datasets/jhu-clsp/CLERC) (Hou et al., *CLERC: A Dataset for U.S. Legal Case Retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Analysis Generation*, Findings of NAACL 2025). The cached vectors are OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small` output.

Everything else in this folder is TypeSafe's work under the repository's MIT licence.
