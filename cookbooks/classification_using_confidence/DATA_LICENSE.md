# Data licence

`filings.jsonl` holds short excerpts (Item 1, "Business") from 10-K annual reports filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission and publicly available on [EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar). Each record's `accession` number identifies the original filing. The text belongs to the filing companies and is not covered by this repository's MIT licence.

`sic_codes.tsv` is the SEC's [SIC code list](https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/division-of-corporation-finance-standard-industrial-classification-sic-code-list), a U.S. government work in the public domain. Source: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the SEC.

Everything else in this folder is TypeSafe's work under the repository's MIT licence.
