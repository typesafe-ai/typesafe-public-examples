# Data licence

`requests.jsonl` holds requests from the [Tip of the Tongue Known-Item Retrieval Dataset for Movie Identification](https://github.com/microsoft/Tip-of-the-Tongue-Known-Item-Retrieval-Dataset-for-Movie-Identification), published by Microsoft under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and described in Arguello et al., "Tip of the Tongue Known-Item Retrieval: A Case Study in Movie Identification" (CHIIR 2021). It is a sample of the dataset's requests with their whitespace collapsed, and each line keeps the URL of the forum thread it came from. `requests.jsonl` is released under CC BY-SA 4.0.

`films.jsonl` holds the title, year and the opening of the plot section of English Wikipedia articles, with the markup removed. Each line links the article it came from. Wikipedia text is available under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and `films.jsonl` is released under the same licence. The list of films to include came from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing), released under CC0 1.0.

The other files this cookbook publishes to typesafe-public-examples are TypeSafe's work under that repository's MIT licence.
