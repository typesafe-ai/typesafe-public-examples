# Data licence

`wikipedia_politeness.json` holds the Wikipedia half of the Stanford Politeness Corpus (Danescu-Niculescu-Mizil, Sudhof, Jurafsky, Leskovec and Potts, "A computational approach to politeness with application to social factors", ACL 2013), as distributed by [ConvoKit](https://convokit.cornell.edu/documentation/wiki_politeness.html) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Each row keeps the request's id, its text and its normalized politeness score; the parses and per-annotator labels are left out.

The request texts are messages that Wikipedia editors left on each other's talk pages, by Wikipedia contributors, licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The other files this cookbook publishes to typesafe-public-examples are TypeSafe's work under that repository's MIT licence.
