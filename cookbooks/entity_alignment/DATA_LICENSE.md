# Data licence

`candidate_pairs.json` pairs album records from [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing) and [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/doc/MusicBrainz_Database), both released under CC0 1.0.

The other files this cookbook publishes to typesafe-public-examples are TypeSafe's work under that repository's MIT licence.
